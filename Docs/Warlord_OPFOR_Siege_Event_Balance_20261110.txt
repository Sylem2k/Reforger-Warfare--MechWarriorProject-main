MY OBJECTIVE
------------
I want a long, fair fight for a 128-player milsim event. TOS-1 and M777
artillery must hurt the boss without destroying it in seconds. The
Warlord should focus on vehicles, destroy tanks before their crews,
and give infantry time to use cover instead of instantly killing them.

WEAPONS AND TARGETING
---------------------
My main Warlord AI stations now choose an unclaimed visible enemy
vehicle first, then a claimed visible vehicle, then dismounted infantry.
I preserved the enemy-faction, distance, existing LOS and burst checks.

My 12 extra Omni turrets now default to vehicle-only targeting.
I added m_bAllowInfantry to the Warlord Omni script to permit later
tuning if desired, but set it OFF on Omni cannon and laser prefabs.
The normal boss arm/AC/LRM banks can still fire on infantry at much
lower damage. Dedicated AA continues attacking aircraft.

On all relevant Warlord gun prefabs, I set the CGS crew-damage shares
for kinetic and explosive damage to 0 and disabled CGS's additional
forced crew killing when vehicles are destroyed. Native vehicle
destruction mechanics still apply. I increased anti-vehicle damage
and armor piercing of the Omni cannons/lasers, AC10, LRM10/15, arm
cannons and flak while reducing their infantry damage substantially.

I reduced the two Warlord-only lascannon arms from their inherited
380 RPM to 90 RPM, scaled infantry hits down to 4% of base damage,
and disabled their legacy true-damage fallback. Anti-vehicle armor
piercing is enabled independently. The stomper now deals 20 instead
of 75 damage to infantry and 850 instead of 450 to vehicles.
